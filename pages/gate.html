Bank · 20 questions

GATE-style

1. A test is positive in 95% of diseased and 5% of healthy people. Disease prevalence is 1%. P(disease | positive) is closest to:

Bayes: (0.95×0.01)/(0.95×0.01 + 0.05×0.99) ≈ 0.161. Base rates dominate.

2. Matrix A (m×n) has rank r. Dimension of its null space is:

Rank–nullity: rank + nullity = n (columns).

3. If X, Y independent with Var(X)=2, Var(Y)=3, then Var(2X − Y) equals:

4·Var(X) + Var(Y) = 8 + 3 = 11. Coefficients square; minus becomes plus.

4. Eigenvectors of a real symmetric matrix corresponding to distinct eigenvalues are:

Spectral theorem: symmetric ⇒ orthonormal eigenbasis. PCA relies on it.

5. A* with an admissible (never overestimating) heuristic is guaranteed to return:

Admissible h ⇒ f(n) never overestimates ⇒ first goal popped is optimal.

6. Alpha-beta pruning, in the worst case, explores:

Best case O(b^d/2) with perfect ordering; worst case no pruning at all.

7. Modus ponens: from P and P → Q, infer:

The oldest inference rule in logic.

8. Increasing model complexity typically: training error ↓, test error ↓ then ↑. The rising part is:

Classic U-curve: bias falls, variance rises. (Double descent aside.)

9. L1 regularization (lasso) tends to produce weight vectors that are:

Diamond constraint hits corners ⇒ feature selection. L2 shrinks smoothly.

10. A hard-margin SVM finds the hyperplane that:

Max margin ⇔ min ||w|| subject to yᵢ(w·xᵢ+b) ≥ 1.

11. k-means is guaranteed to:

Monotone WCSS decrease ⇒ convergence; init decides which optimum (k-means++).

12. PCA’s first principal component is the direction of:

Variance-maximizing projection; standardise features first.

13. A 2-hidden-layer MLP with only linear activations is equivalent to:

No activation ⇒ no depth benefit. Nonlinearity is the whole game.

14. Self-attention over n tokens costs (time/memory in n):

The n×n attention matrix. Why context windows cost money.

15. A conv layer: 3×3 kernel, 16 in-channels, 32 out-channels, bias. Parameter count:

k²·C_in·C_out weights + C_out biases. Input size doesn’t matter.

16. LSTMs mitigate the vanishing gradient mainly via:

Gradients flow down the cell “highway” instead of repeated squashing.

17. ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY cust ORDER BY ts DESC) = 1 picks, per customer:

The standard dedupe/latest-record pattern. RANK would keep ties.

18. A p-value of 0.03 means (correctly):

P(data|H0), not P(H0|data). Report effect + CI alongside.

19. After a LEFT JOIN, row count exceeds the left table’s. Cause:

Each left row matches k right rows ⇒ ×k fanout. Dedupe or pre-aggregate.

20. Min-max scaling vs standardization: min-max is preferred when:

Bounded inputs suit sigmoid-era nets and distance metrics; outliers break it — robust-scale then.
